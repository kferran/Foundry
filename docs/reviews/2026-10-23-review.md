## 1. Verdict

The build process is now disciplined: CI runs the gate on every merge (green on every master push this week), ratchets stop copied parts, and Work Orders deliver real work on outside repositories. The owner's daily product did not improve with it. Today's brief was lost to a Now-page rejection, the third in eight days, while the fix for exactly that (#299) waited behind factory phases F0 to F4. The brief grew to its largest size, the Now page grew by about 25 open lines a day, and the core line budget was raised every time a feature needed room. The owner is away from 10-23 to 11-04, so the next two weeks are a test of the system running unattended.

## 4. Findings

### 4.1 The brief is still lost to one bad Now line (high)

- 10-23 has no brief. The intraday log records a brief-rejection bug report that morning, and #365/#366 (merged today) add `now.py fit-staged`, which cuts a brief-added Now line over the 300-character cap before publish.
- The survey counts two earlier brief rejections over the Now page (10-16, 10-18). Issue #299 ("one bad Now line or a conflict means no brief and no retry") has been open since 10-21.
- The factory plan ranks the factory first and puts #299 on the safety track "alongside". In practice F0 to F4 merged (about 30 PRs in three days) and #299 did not.
- The 300-character cap came from phase A. A rule the model can break in one line was enforced at the gate as a whole-run failure, so a formatting rule cost the day's main output.
- The 10-21 debrief is also missing from the vault's history. The cause is not in the files read for this review.

**Recommendation:** finish #299 before any other phase. Every gate rule a model can break in one line needs a fixer before publish or a drop-the-line fallback, never a rejected run. A brief that cannot publish whole should still publish what passed, with a `now` alert naming what was cut.

### 4.2 The 8 KB brief test is green and the live brief is 39 KB (high)

- `prep.bats` "brief size" renders the prep inputs on a synthetic vault and checks they sum under 8 KB. It passes in CI.
- The live 10-22 brief is 39.0 KB. Measured by section: Morning Alignment 14.3 KB, Blockers 13.7 KB, Active Projects 5.3 KB, Waiting on 3.4 KB.
- Blockers → Pipeline alone is 9.8 KB. Most of it reprints the previous day's `[watch]` alerts line by line, though the today file already holds them. The intraday manifest gives `watch` the severity `now`, and the brief prints every alert line of the day before.
- The model's own sections add more: "The week so far" (4.6 KB on a Thursday), Overdue (2.6 KB), the owner's meeting actions (2.8 KB).
- Two smoke lines ask to re-run the acceptance steps after each `claude` patch release (two releases in one morning).

**Recommendation:** measure the output, on the live vault. A daily check on the server that alerts when the published brief passes 8 KB is worth more than the fixture test. Route `watch` alerts to the today file only, and print a count in the brief. Report a `claude` version change once a week, unless a smoke check fails.

### 4.3 The Now page grows faster than it closes (medium; urgent with the owner away)

- 169 open work lines, from 96 a week ago. Added: 26 on 10-21, 23 on 10-22, 36 on 10-23.
- The survey found that most lines come from mail and chat triage and that almost nothing is ticked since 10-16 (120 of 254 closed lines were dismissed, 15 closed on evidence).
- At today's rate the page holds about 400 open lines when the owner returns on 11-04.

**Recommendation:** before the absence matters, cap machine-added lines per day, fold machine-added lines nobody touched for 5 days into one weekly digest line, and let the away rollup (#264) summarize the absence. Closing on evidence (15 of 254) is the part of the Now page that saves the owner time; extend it before adding sources.

### 4.4 The core budget does not constrain (medium)

- The core line budget in `vault_integrity.bats` was set at the end of phase 4 on 10-16 and raised in the same pull request as each feature that needed room: nine raises, 12,500 to 15,000, removed on 10-21 and restored on 10-22 over a wider scope.
- Total code doubled since 10-11 (10,887 to 22,218 lines) while the program was named "simplify the template".
- Three extensions moved out (handoffs, telemetry with RCA, clearing-house to the vault only). Meetings, triage, notices and sync stayed in core by decision on 10-16, and the intraday extension is new code.

**Recommendation:** decide which goal holds. If the template is a product for other owners, freeze the cap and make a raise need a removal of the same size. If it is the owner's own system, retire the "about half the code" target from the roadmap so the numbers stop reading as misses.

### 4.5 Open confinement and safety issues sit behind feature work (medium)

- The two #298 high findings merged (#323, #324, #328, #330). Section 5 holds the independent check of those fixes.

**Recommendation:** while the owner is away, merge only the safety track and #299.

### 4.6 Built and unused (low)

- The survey's other unused parts are unchanged: the Notes block, the personal partition, the RCA filer (0 tickets).

**Recommendation:** retire the friction flag on entities with a lint rule, or retire the friction section. Hold new features until the unused ones are used or removed.

## 5. Code check of this week's safety fixes

An independent read of `master` at a7af750, done without the specs or PR descriptions as truth. It covered publish through symlinks (#323, #328), the Work Order re-screen (#324, #330), the `private/` folder (#258) and the connector library and extension API (#329, #350, #352). One probe was run; the suites were not.

**Sound:**
- Publish: every path the gate takes in goes through `_resolves`, and the run's records are checked with `safe_rel`, the staging-path match, the private pattern and `target_matches`. There is a window between validation and the staged file's rewrite (`publish.py:566-572`), but no flow leaves a writer running while the gate runs.
- `private/`: the index, `inside_vault`, `show`, `related`, `target_matches` and the research context all exclude it.
- The connector library: `walk` refuses unlisted tools, `too_large` cannot be faked from JSON text, and a missing result fails.

**Findings**, highest first:

| # | Severity | Where | What |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | medium | `vaultlib/notify.py:253`, `:314` | A Work Order notice carries the first "Needs you" line, which a research session can write after reading web pages. The post check allows the posted text to be up to 500 characters longer than what was collected (`EXTRA_TEXT`), so an injected question can post new text as the owner, a mention or a link included. Checked in the code for this review. |
| C2 | medium-low | `vaultlib/nightshift_run.py:652` (in `_screen`) | The tick re-runs the field screen (`nc.screen`) but not the plan checks. A queue note edited after `add` (`tasks`, `base`, `plan`) can run task blocks the add-time credential and protected-path checks would refuse. `model`, `session_id` (passed to `--resume`) and a codebase's `order_hosts` are screened at neither time. The session holds no credentials, which limits the damage. |
| C3 | medium-low | `vaultlib/nightshift_run.py:264`, `:289` | A resumed research item keeps its old `out/` and re-checks out `code/` at the recorded commit without checking `repo`. If `repo` or `partition` changed between attempts, findings from the old codebase publish under the new partition. |
| C4 | medium-low, not run | `vaultlib/nightshift_session.py:24` | Work Order sessions deny `~/.claude/projects` and the settings files, but not `~/.claude/history.jsonl` or `~/.claude/file-history/`, which hold typed prompts and snapshots of edited files, `private/` notes possibly among them. Research sessions also have web fetch to their brief's hosts. |
| C5 | low, probe run | `vaultlib/triage.py:186-206` | Mail triage reads a search call with no tool result, or a result without `structuredContent`, as an empty inbox, and moves the cursor, so that window's mail is never triaged. The ticket and Drive extractors fail in the same case. |
| C6 | low | `calendar_tsv.py:103`, `:113` | Calendar events come from the model's structured output and are never matched against the tool results, so text in an invite can add or hide events in the brief. The meetings search accepts any search arguments the same way. |
| C7 | low | `extensions/telemetry/scripts/telemetrylib/rca.py:167` | `reopen` writes tracker error text into a Now line without `now.validate`; a `[due:: …]` in that text becomes the line's due date. |

Two small gaps beside these: `_research_dir` checks only the last folder for a symlink, so a symlinked `wiki/<p>` is followed (`nightshift_run.py:271`); and nothing stops a registered codebase whose `path` is the vault itself, which a plan Work Order would clone with its private history.

**Recommendation:** C1 first, because it is the one path from outside text to a message posted as the owner: post exactly the collected text (or only escaped text plus fixed links), and treat a mismatch as a failed post. C2 and C3 fit with #321. C5 should fail like the other extractors.
