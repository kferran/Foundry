# Now

Open loops: what you owe, what you wait on, and messages not yet sent. Tick a line when it is done, or mark it `[-]` to drop it. Each line is `- [ ] <owed|waiting|draft>: <statement>[ [rec:: <option>: <why>]][ [due:: YYYY-MM-DD]] (<who>, since <date>[, <evidence>])`: `rec` is the writer's recommendation and `due` the date to settle it by; keep both before the parenthesis. A line past its `due` shows under Overdue in the brief.
